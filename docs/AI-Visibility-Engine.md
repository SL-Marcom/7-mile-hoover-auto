# AI Visibility Engine

## Purpose

This engine helps SiteLab create content that is easy for both people and AI systems to understand, summarize, and trust.

It extends the existing Content Intelligence Engine and SEO Intelligence Engine rather than replacing them.

## Scope

The engine covers:
- answer-first content structure
- entity clarity and consistency
- business, service, location, and person entity relationships
- citation-ready factual content
- source and evidence requirements
- FAQ and question-based content strategy
- concise summaries and direct answers
- semantic coverage and topical completeness
- AI-friendly page structure
- structured data recommendations that extend the SEO engine
- knowledge graph consistency
- content freshness and update signals
- unsupported-claim detection
- AI visibility review checklists
- approval gates

## Integration model

The AI Visibility Engine should consume the content blueprint and SEO blueprint where relevant so it stays aligned with page intent, audience, brand voice, and structured data planning.

It should not duplicate content strategy or SEO implementation tasks. Instead, it should clarify how the content should be framed so it is easier to understand, summarize, and cite.

## Core operating rules

- Lead with the clearest answer to the user’s question.
- Keep entity names consistent and connected to the right facts.
- Support claims with approved evidence or source notes.
- Use FAQs and clear structure to cover likely questions.
- Keep the page easy to summarize without losing the core meaning.
- Treat unsupported claims as review items rather than approved facts.

## Approval gates

A page should not be considered AI visibility ready until it passes:
- answer-first structure review,
- entity clarity review,
- source and evidence review,
- FAQ and summary review,
- structured data alignment review,
- and final AI visibility approval.

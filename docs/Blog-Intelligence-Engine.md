# Blog Intelligence Engine

The Blog Intelligence Engine is a shared planning layer for blog content inside the SiteLab operating system.

## Purpose

It extends the existing Content Intelligence, SEO Intelligence, AI Visibility, Local SEO, and Industry Knowledge engines so blog planning stays connected to the same review and approval system rather than becoming a parallel workflow.

## What it supports

- blog strategy and editorial planning
- pillar and supporting content planning
- keyword and search-intent mapping
- audience and funnel-stage alignment
- industry-general topic and question suggestions from the Industry Knowledge Engine, pending client confirmation
- article briefs and title planning
- article structure and writing guidance
- natural, client-specific writing guidance
- fact verification and evidence requirements
- internal linking to services, locations, and related articles
- schema recommendations and FAQ opportunities
- image and featured-image guidance
- metadata and canonical strategy
- local relevance where appropriate
- AI visibility optimization
- overlap and cannibalization checks
- freshness and update planning
- repurposing opportunities
- review checklists and approval gates

## Guardrails

- Do not invent facts, statistics, quotes, credentials, case studies, results, or client experience.
- Require public research and owner approval before using business-specific claims.
- Support both evergreen and timely content.
- Do not publish, deploy, schedule, or connect a CMS.

## Integration pattern

The engine consumes the existing blueprint patterns from the shared content, SEO, AI visibility, and local SEO systems, then returns a reusable blog blueprint plus a review evaluation.

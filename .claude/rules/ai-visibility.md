# AI Visibility Guidance

Make content understandable to both users and AI systems without relying on spammy tactics.

- Write clear, factual, well-structured content with explicit service names and page intent.
- Use semantic headings, concise paragraphs, and direct answers to likely user questions.
- Make entity names, services, and location references accurate and consistent.
- Avoid hidden text, keyword stuffing, or misleading claims.
- Prefer content that is genuinely useful and easy to summarize.
- Do not fabricate credentials, achievements, or endorsements to improve perceived authority.
- Apply the SiteLab AI Visibility Engine for answer-first structure, entity clarity, citation-ready facts, source requirements, FAQ strategy, concise summaries, semantic coverage, structured-data alignment, knowledge consistency, freshness signals, unsupported-claim detection, review checklists, and approval gates.
- Use the AI Visibility Engine together with the Content Intelligence Engine and SEO Intelligence Engine so content strategy, SEO structure, and AI readability stay aligned.
- Use the Industry Knowledge Engine for common customer questions and category terminology suggestions, and treat them as FAQ and entity starting points that still require client confirmation.
- Preserve extension points for future Local SEO and Blog Intelligence engines.
- Route the final AI visibility check through the Reviewer/QA Engine, which aggregates this engine's evaluation instead of duplicating it.
- Source business, service, and location entities from the Client Intake Engine's client-confirmed fields, never from research-only or assumed values.

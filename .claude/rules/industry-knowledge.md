# Industry Knowledge Guidance

Industry knowledge is category-general suggestion data, not client-specific fact.

- Use the SiteLab Industry Knowledge Engine for lightweight industry classification, reusable industry profiles, terminology suggestions, typical services, common customer questions, typical trust signals, compliance sensitivity, seasonal patterns, and design tone direction.
- Treat every classification as a suggestion with an explicit confidence level, never a determination of fact about the client.
- Require explicit client confirmation before using any industry-derived terminology, service, question, or trust signal in published content.
- Flag compliance-sensitive categories (for example medical and legal) so claims and guarantees get extra scrutiny.
- If no industry profile confidently matches, say so and default to neutral, cautious language rather than forcing a guess.
- Use the Industry Knowledge Engine together with the Content Intelligence Engine, SEO Intelligence Engine, AI Visibility Engine, Local SEO Intelligence Engine, Blog Intelligence Engine, and Design Intelligence Engine so category guidance stays aligned with approved voice, structure, and local context.
- Do not use this engine to generate full-site presets, page structure, or final copy. It supplies suggestions only.

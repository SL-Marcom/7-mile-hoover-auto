# Local SEO Guidance

For local-business sites, optimize for trust, clarity, and local relevance.

- Only include local business details when they are provided or explicitly approved.
- If details are missing, use placeholders instead of inventing them.
- Keep names, addresses, phone numbers, service areas, hours, and directions consistent across visible content and structured data.
- Prioritize the most useful action for the business: call, request a quote, book, or get directions.
- Use location-aware page copy only when it is accurate and relevant.
- Avoid implying coverage, credentials, or service areas that have not been confirmed.
- Preserve local intent without overstating geographic reach.

- Use the Local SEO engine together with the Content Intelligence Engine, SEO Intelligence Engine, and AI Visibility Engine so content, SEO, and local relevance stay aligned.
- Use the Industry Knowledge Engine for suggested business-category options and typical trust signals, and confirm the actual category and details with the client before using them in local content or schema.
- Preserve extension points for future Blog Intelligence, Client Intake, and Deployment engines.
- Route the final local SEO check through the Reviewer/QA Engine, which aggregates this engine's evaluation instead of duplicating it.
- Source the business model, primary location, and service areas from the Client Intake Engine's client-confirmed fields, and treat a contradiction it reports (for example a storefront model with no confirmed location) as a hard blocker.

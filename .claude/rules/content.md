# Content Guidance

All copy must be factual, specific, and safe to publish.

- Never invent business information, reviews, ratings, awards, credentials, prices, addresses, years of experience, service areas, guarantees, or promises.
- If client information is missing, use clearly marked placeholders such as [Client Name], [Phone Number], [Service Area], or [Address].
- Prefer concise, useful copy that helps a visitor decide what to do next.
- Match the content to the client’s real services and local context.
- Use plain language and direct calls to action.
- Avoid vague filler, generic marketing language, and fabricated proof points.
- Keep each page focused on one primary purpose: call, form, booking, or directions.
- Apply the SiteLab Content Intelligence Engine for website pages, service pages, location pages, landing pages, blogs, FAQs, GBP posts, emails, and social content.
- Apply the Blog Intelligence Engine for editorial planning, topic clusters, article briefs, headline planning, article structure, internal-link planning, FAQ ideas, metadata strategy, freshness planning, repurposing opportunities, and review checklists.
- Use the Industry Knowledge Engine for category-general terminology, typical services, and common customer questions, and treat every suggestion as pending client confirmation rather than approved content.
- Use the Human Writing Standard to avoid unnecessary em dashes, repetitive AI phrasing, generic filler, exaggerated assertions, fake facts, and repetitive sentence rhythm.
- Favor concrete language, varied sentence length, natural transitions, client-specific terminology, and the approved brand voice.
- Require research, fact verification, trust-signal review, internal-link review, SEO compatibility review, AI visibility compatibility review, and approval before treating content as publish-ready.
- Route the final quality check through the Reviewer/QA Engine, which aggregates this engine's evaluation instead of duplicating it.
- Source the audience, brand voice, trust signals, and internal links this engine needs from the Client Intake Engine's client-confirmed fields, never from research-only or assumed values.
